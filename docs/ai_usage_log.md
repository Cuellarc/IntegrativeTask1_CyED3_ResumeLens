## 2. Use of Generative AI

### 2.1. Implementation and Troubleshooting

The team led the development and used GenAI to explore implementation approaches, understand their effects on the code, and investigate errors. Suggestions were evaluated against project requirements before the team selected and implemented corrections.

**Prompts:**

- “What changes would each approach require in the code, and what are the advantages and disadvantages?”
- “Analyze these error logs, explain the likely cause of the failure, and suggest possible solutions.”
- “Review this implementation and identify potential problems with its behavior or integration with the rest of the project.”

### 2.2. Documentation and Research

We were responsible for preparing the project documentation and ensuring that the information was consistent with our implementation. GenAI helped us organize and format design documents, improve wording and grammar, and structure the literature review. We supplied project-specific information, reviewed the suggestions, and checked the final content against the implementation and requirements.

The team also developed a generator for `docs/transducer_catalog.md`. It reads the transducer definitions and produces their seven-tuple representations and transition diagrams for all 41 transducers.

**Prompts:**

- “Help organize this design document into clear sections and use a consistent technical format.”
- “Improve the grammar and clarity of this paragraph without changing its technical meaning.”
- “Help organize these bibliographic references and summarize how each source relates to our project.”

### 2.3. Research Poster

The team selected the format and layout of the research poster. GenAI helped improve its organization, readability, and presentation. The team reviewed and adjusted the final design.

**Prompt:**

- “Suggest improvements to the layout and readability of this research poster while keeping the project information accurate and easy to understand.”

## 3. Team Organization and Contributions

The team divided the work between the project stages and communicated which sections each member planned to work on to reduce conflicts. Development used separate Git branches, consistent commit-message conventions, and pull requests. The team decided to develop the graphical interface after completing the main functional components.

The team made the project decisions, selected the additional profiles and optional report fields, reviewed component integration, and performed version-control operations, including commits, pushes, and pull requests. GenAI supported analysis and suggestions, but the team remained responsible for selecting and applying changes.

## 4. Verification and Results

The team reviewed AI suggestions, investigated test failures using error logs, corrected issues, and checked that the documentation matched the actual implementation. At the time of recording, **115 tests were passing**. The team also reviewed component integration, bibliographic information, the graphical interface, and the research poster.

## 5. Responsibility and Understanding

GenAI was a support tool, not a replacement for the team's work. We are capable of explaining the technical concepts and implementation decisions involved in our work. The team was responsible for reviewing suggestions, deciding which ones to use, implementing corrections, and verifying the final results.
